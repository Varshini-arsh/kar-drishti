Office Circular No. PRG/ADM/7/2022
Dated: 1 July 2022
Subject: Revised leave guidelines for staff of the demo office

1. An officer may avail up to 15 days of casual leave in a calendar year.
2. Up to 5 days of unavailed casual leave may be carried forward to the next calendar year.
3. Earned leave may be accumulated up to a maximum of 60 days.
4. Work from home is permitted for up to 2 days in a month with the prior approval of the controlling officer.
5. Applications for leave shall be submitted at least 3 working days in advance.

(sd/-) Head of Office (demo)
