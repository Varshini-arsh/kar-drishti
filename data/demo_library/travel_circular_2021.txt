Office Circular No. PRG/ADM/4/2021
Dated: 10 August 2021
Subject: Tour and travel rules for staff of the demo office

1. An advance of up to 80% of the estimated cost of the tour may be sanctioned.
2. Officers are entitled to travel by AC 2-tier.
3. Daily allowance (DA) is payable at Rs 900 per day of tour.
4. Travel claims shall be submitted within 15 days of completion of the tour.
