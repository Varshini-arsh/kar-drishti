The three circulars in this folder are FICTITIOUS demo documents written to test Pragya.
They are not real government circulars or office orders.

section_map_1961_2025.csv is an EMPTY template. Pragya looks up sections between the
Income-tax Act, 1961 and the Income-tax Act, 2025 ONLY from this table and never guesses.
Fill it from the mapping issued by the Department (or add your own file with Add data,
columns: section_1961, section_2025, subject, source_document).
