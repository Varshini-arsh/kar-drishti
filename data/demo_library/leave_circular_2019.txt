Office Circular No. PRG/ADM/12/2019
Dated: 15 March 2019
Subject: Leave rules for staff of the demo office

1. An officer may avail up to 12 days of casual leave in a calendar year.
2. Unavailed casual leave shall lapse at the end of the calendar year and cannot be carried forward.
3. Earned leave may be accumulated up to a maximum of 30 days.
4. Work from home is not permitted.
5. Applications for leave shall be submitted at least 3 working days in advance.

(sd/-) Head of Office (demo)
